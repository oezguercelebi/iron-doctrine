## Notes on accuracy

Point-tree numbers (para-drop squad sizes, cash-hack amounts, artillery shell counts) disagree slightly across GameSpot, StrategyWiki, and the C&C Wiki. Treat them as **order of magnitude** until we read the INI/XML in a later pass. Roles and faction holes are consistent across sources.
